Why a dachshund can interrupt a panic faster than a lecture

A long body on a lap is weight, warmth, and a job for the hands. The nervous system often takes the animal before it takes the explanation. Sausage Therapy uses that order on purpose: dog first, words second, plan third. It is not magic and it is not a diagnosis. It is a reliable interrupt.

What we will not pretend

The dogs are not clinicians. Handlers do not replace psychologists, occupational therapists, or support coordinators. If someone is in crisis, the next step is local emergency or a registered service. The empire gets stronger by staying inside the truth.

Forevermore is the long game

Sessions pay for feed, vet, fences, and the next scholarship visit. Membership pays for the weeks the road is closed. The journal pays in trust. Together they turn a true practice into a tremendous one without abandoning the dogs that started it.
